Privacy Policy — Cart Health Guardian

This privacy policy applies to the "Cart Health Guardian" browser extension, published by Zovo (operated by Autom8 LLC). Effective 7 July 2026, updated 5 August 2026.

This page is the dedicated privacy policy for the Cart Health Guardian Chrome extension. It describes exactly what information the extension handles, what never leaves your device, and your rights.

1. Summary

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Ever.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking networks in our extensions.
  • Most extension functionality runs 100% on your device; your browsing content, page data, screenshots, and recordings stay local and are never uploaded to us.
  • The only data that leaves your device is (a) a license key you enter, sent to our license server to verify a Pro purchase, and (b) payment details you provide on our website's checkout, handled by our payment processor.

3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your browsing history, the content of pages you visit, keystrokes, form data, screenshots, screen recordings, or personally identifying information, except the minimal license/payment data described above that you actively provide. We do not build advertising profiles.

4. Permissions

Each extension requests the minimum Chrome permissions required for its stated function, and justifies each in its Chrome Web Store listing. Permissions are used solely to deliver the feature you installed the extension for — not to gather data. Where possible we use activeTab and optional permissions you grant on demand.

5. Third parties

We use a small number of processors strictly to operate the service: our license-verification backend (Supabase) to validate Pro keys, and Stripe to process website payments. We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. AI features use a provider you choose with your key; that provider's privacy policy governs those requests.

6. Data retention

Local settings persist until you clear them or uninstall the extension. License verification records are retained for the life of the license to keep your Pro access working. You can remove all local data any time by removing the extension.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the limited personal data we hold (your license key and associated purchase record), and you may object to or restrict its processing. We do not sell personal information. To exercise any right, email support@zovo.one and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the effective date above. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.
